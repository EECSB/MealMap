      Keep your recipes, note what you have in, plan the week, and let the shopping list build itself from the plan — it adds up quantities and leaves out what is already in your pantry.       Data & sharing

      Your data lives in this browser

      There is no account and no server — nothing leaves this device unless you set up sync below. Clearing your browsing data, switching browser, or moving to another device starts you over. Keep a copy: export a file, or link one below and it stays up to date on its own.

      Export your meals and meal-plan as a file, then drop it in Google Drive or OneDrive to back it up or share it.

      JSON keeps everything exactly; Excel is a readable copy. Import replaces current data.

      💾 Keep a file up to date

      Pick a file in a folder your cloud already syncs — OneDrive, Dropbox, Drive, iCloud — and this keeps it current. No account and no setup: whatever syncs that folder does the rest.

      This needs Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer — other browsers do not offer it yet. Use Export and Import above, or OneDrive below.

      ☁ OneDrive live sync

      Sign in to keep everyone on the same data automatically. Needs one-time setup: a hosted link and a Microsoft app ID.
